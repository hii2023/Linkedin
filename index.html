Put it on LinkedIn

  1. Save the image from the preview panel.
  2. Open your LinkedIn profile and click your profile photo.
  3. Choose to change or upload a new photo, pick the saved PNG and keep the ring inside the circle.

For Open to Work and Hiring, LinkedIn also has its own built-in frames that switch on recruiter and job search visibility. A frame drawn into your photo only changes how you look, so turn those settings on as well.
